# Lantern v0.11 ambience candidates

The five bundled soundscapes are original procedural, non-musical ambience created for Lantern testing; no third-party audio assets are embedded.

Files:
- rain_glass.wav
- night_room.wav
- open_air.wav
- journey.wav
- quiet_fire.wav

They are intentionally restrained and loop underneath narration or reading. Treat them as customer-facing production candidates for the Closed Alpha, not as a permanent final sound library. Audition them on headphones and phone speakers before inviting testers.
